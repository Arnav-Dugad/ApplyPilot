# Privacy

ApplyPilot is local-first. Profile facts, application answers, job snapshots, CV metadata, and receipts are stored on the user's machine. Ollama is optional and off by default. No OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, paid API, telemetry service, or hosted ApplyPilot account is required.

Future integrations must be opt-in, use least-privilege OAuth, disclose exactly which data leaves the device, and support revocation. Export, backup, restore, and deletion will operate on a user-chosen archive without silently retaining copies.
